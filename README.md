# Partner in justice

> "a person or entity that collaborates with another to uphold the law, solve crimes, and ensure fairness within the legal system. "

## Characters

*   **baek beom:** A forensic doctor with ten years of experience at the National Forensic Service.
*   **stella hwang:** who is a work colleague of Baek Beom at the National Institute of Scientific Investigation.
*   **eun sol:** rookie prosecutor with a warm heart. She has a bright personality and she comes from wealth family background.
*   **cha soo-ho:**  detective, watches the procedure from behind a glass. He mentions the disappearance of the victim's.
*   **kang hyun:** hostile relationship with Eun Sol's “teammate” Baek Beom, one that was caused by a mysterious event.
